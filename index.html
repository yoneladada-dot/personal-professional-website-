About

Two starting points, one craft.

I am a Literacy Facilitator with experience in teaching, language development, and contact centre operations. I work at two important starting points: where a child reads their first word, and where a professional speaks their first confident sentence to a customer.

I use Zoom, Google Classroom, and guided WhatsApp lessons to make learning accessible, engaging, and measurable. My approach is learner-first, tech-enabled, and people-centred.

Little Readers Digital Literacy Program

A blended literacy program for Foundation Phase learners (Grades R–3), combining live Zoom lessons with Google Classroom activities and WhatsApp voice-note practice for parents. Improved reading confidence in 8 weeks.

  • Google Classroom

02

Contact Centre Communication Accelerator

A 4-week coaching program for new contact centre agents focusing on call structure, de-escalation, and professional language. Includes simulated calls, recording reviews, and daily WhatsApp micro-lessons.

  • Zoom
  • Call Simulation Scripts
  • Google Sheets (QA Scorecards)
  • WhatsApp Business

WhatsApp Literacy Bridge

A low-data literacy support initiative for parents to continue learning at home. Bite-size phonics videos, printable worksheets, and voice-note feedback to keep children learning during school breaks.
